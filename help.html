Frequently Asked Questions

How can I change my password?

Go to the "Password" tab from the sidebar menu, enter your current password, and type your new secure password.

How to export customer reports?

Navigate to the "Customers" page, apply your desired filters, and click on the "View All" or export button near the table header to download data.

Is this admin panel fully mobile friendly?

Yes! Every single grid, card, sidebar navigation, and datatable has been carefully structured to adapt smoothly on smartphone viewports.
